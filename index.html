Why This Matters

Destruction of Kingsteignton's last large area of mature woodland habitat (protected bats, dormice, birds).

50 years of harmful silica dust, noise and HGV traffic beside homes and schools.

Tree removal strips natural flood buffering — 2024 already saw the worst local flooding in decades.

Conflicts with Teignbridge Local Plan (EN2A, EN8–EN12) and Devon Minerals Plan M6 safeguarding landscape, habitats and community.

Pre-planning objections open now

Sibelco have opened the pre-planning objections form as part of their planning application. Please complete this now and share widely. Use this text in the “How will this impact you” box:

  • Destruction of Kingsteignton's last large area of mature forest, removing habitat for owls, birds, bats, badgers and deer.
  • Two schools lie downwind — silica dust risks children’s health (asthma, cancer).
  • Noise pollution will ruin our peaceful community.
  • You can never deliver a genuine 110% net gain of mature broadleaf forest.
  • HGV traffic and noise will impact quality of life.
  • River Teign water quality downstream will inevitably suffer.
  • Removing forest increases flood risk.
